Arrow Tracker (Python Simulation)

This is a simulation of my Python webserver! It develops a 52 week training cycle, just like my python script does.
The script presents an "expected" training volume for each day, based on a base volume, and builds on that each week. Once arrow volumes are entered, the number of arrows shot, and the scores recorded appear on the calendar. If the training volume for that day is met, the day turns green, but if training volume differs by 20 arrows or more, the day turns red.
Feel free to add entries to try it out!

Dartfish

I have also learned to use Dartfish for video analysis! Here is an example of some analysis I did on my own shooting.

In the full video, I examined my posture and determined that I was leaning back while at full draw. Ideally, an archer should be standing straight up, for maximum stability. This backward lean caused the angle between my shoulder and trunk to decrease, which can lead to difficulties keeping the shoulder securely in place. I also noticed that my wrist on my release hand was slightly bent, which can produce excess tension in my shot, and result in inconsistencies in shot execution.
I often use this same analysis when I am coaching athletes, and it is a great tool to help them identify areas of their form and shot process that they can improve upon.
