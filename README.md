## Introduction
Welcome to my website! This is intended to act as a virtual portfolio to demonstrate my skills, as well as a place to share my experiences and interests. There are a few different sections to my website, each with a different purpose.
- **Home**: A brief introduction to me and my work.
- **Work Experience**: A list of my work experience and the skills I have developed.
- **Skills**: A list of my skills and the tools I have learned to use.
- **Archery**: A brief overview of my archery career and achievements.
- **Research**: A list of the research I have been a part of.
- **Achievements and Interests**: An outline of some of my achievements, as well as a look into my interests and hobbies
- **Supporting Documents**: My resume, and other documents that support my work experience and skills.

## Tools Used
- **HTML**: For the structure of the website.
- **CSS**: For the styling of the website.
- **JavaScript**: For the interactive elements of the website.
- **Python**: For the backend of the website.
- **Flask**: For the web framework of the website.
- **SQLite**: For the database of the website.
- **Git**: For version control of the website.
- **GitHub**: For hosting the website.
- **Antigravity Agent**: For AI assistance in development, particularly in some of the back end work, or adaptations of my scripts for web use.

## How to navigate the website
Navigation of this project is relatively simple. It is designed to function as a standard website, with sub pages containing information for each individual section. Links to the subpages can be accessed through the home page. Some subpages have links to other websites, or to download some of my projects as well. At the bottom of the home page, there is also a contact form which will send an email to me.
